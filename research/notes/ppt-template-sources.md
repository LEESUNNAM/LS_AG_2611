# Free PPT Template Sources with Commercial Use (checked 2026-11-06)

Sites that offer free presentation templates, with what each site's own license or terms page says about commercial use. Every row was checked on the site's official license or terms page with the Playwright MCP on 2026-11-06. Blog roundups from the Google results were not used as evidence.

> This is a research note, not legal advice. Site policies change, so read the linked page again before relying on it for a paid project.

## Details

### 1. SlidesCarnival
- URL: https://www.slidescarnival.com/ (terms: https://www.slidescarnival.com/terms-of-use)
- Format: PowerPoint and Google Slides. The home page says "Free for any use, no registration".
- License: "All templates by SlidesCarnival are free to use under Creative Commons License Attribution 4.0 International." The terms allow adapting the material "for any purpose, even commercially".
- Conditions: give appropriate credit and a link to the website. Keep the Credits slide when the template includes photographs, because those have their own licenses.
- Limits: you may not resell, sublicense, or redistribute the files as-is (without modification). A presentation built from a template may be resold or redistributed only if you added your own content.
- Note: the terms page text names Canva as the provider of the service.

### 2. Slidesgo
- URL: https://slidesgo.com/ (terms: https://slidesgo.com/terms-conditions, clause 7)
- Format: Google Slides themes and PowerPoint templates (the home page title). Some templates have extra slides for Premium users only.
- License: clause 7.2 allows Adapted Content in non-editable format in "personal, professional, commercial, educational and advertising or marketing presentations", including presentations intended for sale incorporated in a product.
- Conditions: the attribution section generated by the website must stay in the presentation. Removing it is allowed only with a license that permits omitting attribution (the Premium subscription).
- Limits: no reselling, transferring, or sublicensing of the templates or of editable adapted content. Editable files may be shared only with the team working on the presentation.
- Operator: Freepik Company, S.L.U.

### 3. Canva (Free Content License)
- URL: https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/
- License: Free Content is licensed at no cost (perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide). Permitted uses include advertising and promotional projects and presentations.
- Watch out: Canva labels some content Pro, which is paid, valid for one design, and may not be distributed as a standalone item. Branded Content follows stricter rules, and a design that mixes content types takes the strictest one.
- Not verified: whether an account is required, and whether a given template exports cleanly to `.pptx`.

### 4. MiriCanvas (미리캔버스)
- URL: https://www.miricanvas.com/ (license agreement, effective 2023-09-11: https://www.miricanvas.com/s/119)
- Allowed examples in the agreement: presentation designs for talks or lectures, and corporate promotion or project report designs as non-editable PPT. A downloaded PPT may be edited in PowerPoint in limited ways (move, resize, delete, animate, add pages and text).
- Conditions: for portfolio use, the work must be clearly marked as made with the service.
- Limits: no re-licensing, resale, or redistribution of the content, no posting to stock-image sites or creator markets, and no use as a registered logo or trademark.
- Free-supplier content (공유마당, 공공누리, Pixabay, Pexels) follows each supplier's own license, shown in each item's details.
- Not verified: whether the free plan and the Pro plan differ in permitted commercial use. The license page does not say. Ask the service (miricanvas@miricanvas.com is listed on the page) if a project depends on it.

### 5. Microsoft PowerPoint templates
- URL: https://powerpoint.cloud.microsoft/create/en/presentation-templates/ (redirect target of create.microsoft.com)
- The page offers "free slide templates for presentations". The only terms link on it leads to the Microsoft Terms of Use, which says "Unless otherwise specified, the Services are for your personal and non-commercial use."
- No statement allowing commercial use of the templates was found on the pages read. The Microsoft Services Agreement and any template-specific terms were not read, so treat commercial use as unconfirmed.

## Method and limits

- Search: Google (Korean query "무료 PPT 템플릿 상업적 이용 가능"), then the official license pages of the candidate sites.
- Text on each license page was filtered by keyword (commercial, attribution, resale and similar) and the matching passages were read. Clauses that did not match those keywords were not read in full.
- Slidesgo's first terms URL guess returned 404, so the link from its home page was used instead.
- Screenshots of each license page are kept locally in `output/playwright/` (`ppt-license-*.png`, `ppt-search-google.png`).
- Not covered: Google Slides' own template gallery, Freepik, and Korean services other than MiriCanvas.
